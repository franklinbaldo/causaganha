# User Personas & Journeys

This document describes the key users of CausaGanha and their interaction patterns with the platform.

---

## 👤 Persona 1: Maria - The Legal Consumer (Primary)

### Demographics
- **Age:** 35
- **Occupation:** Elementary school teacher
- **Location:** Porto Velho, Rondônia
- **Income:** R$ 4,500/month
- **Education:** Bachelor's degree
- **Tech Savvy:** Moderate (uses smartphone, social media, Google)

### Context
Maria was recently fired from her teaching position without proper severance pay. She believes she has a strong labor lawsuit case but has never hired a lawyer before. Her budget is limited, and she can't afford to hire the wrong lawyer.

### Goals
1. Find a competent labor lawyer in Rondônia
2. Avoid overpaying for legal services
3. Make an informed decision based on actual performance data
4. Understand the lawyer's track record in similar cases

### Pain Points
- 😟 **Information Overload:** Every lawyer's website claims they're "the best"
- 💸 **Budget Constraints:** Can't afford expensive consultations to evaluate multiple lawyers
- 🤷 **No Trusted Referrals:** Doesn't know anyone who's hired a labor lawyer
- ⏰ **Time Pressure:** Statute of limitations is approaching
- 😰 **Fear of Scams:** Heard stories of lawyers who take money and don't deliver

### Behaviors
- Googles "melhores advogados trabalhistas Rondônia"
- Reads online reviews (but doesn't trust them fully)
- Asks friends/family for recommendations
- Checks OAB registration to verify lawyers are legitimate
- Compares hourly rates and payment terms

### Success Criteria
✅ Finds a lawyer with:
- 60%+ win rate in labor cases
- Experience in Rondônia (TJRO)
- At least 20 cases in the database (sufficient data)
- Affordable fees
- Positive case outcomes in similar situations

## 🚫 Anti-Persona: Judge Silva (NOT a User)

### Who They Are
- **Age:** 55
- **Role:** Labor court judge
- **Experience:** 20 years on the bench

### Why NOT a User
CausaGanha analyzes **lawyer performance**, not judge performance or court efficiency. While judges are mentioned in decisions, we:
- ❌ Don't rate judges
- ❌ Don't analyze judicial bias
- ❌ Don't provide tools for courts
- ❌ Don't optimize court workflows

### Boundary
We analyze **public court data** about **lawyers**, not courts themselves. This keeps our mission focused and avoids conflict with the judiciary.
