# CausaGanha Marketing Strategy

## Brand Positioning

**Tagline**: "Encontre o Advogado Certo para Sua Causa"

**Brand Voice**:
- Trustworthy and transparent
- Data-driven, not opinionated
- Empowering, not judgmental
- Professional but accessible

**Key Messages**:
1. "Decisoes baseadas em dados, nao em marketing"
2. "O primeiro rating de advogados do Brasil"
3. "Dados publicos, resultados reais"

---

## Target Personas

### Persona 1: Maria (B2C - Primary)
- **Age**: 35-55
- **Profile**: Middle-class, facing labor dispute with ex-employer
- **Pain**: Doesn't know how to choose a good lawyer
- **Behavior**: Googles "melhor advogado trabalhista", asks friends
- **Channel**: Google, WhatsApp groups, Facebook

### Persona 2: Roberto (B2B - Secondary)
- **Age**: 40-55
- **Profile**: Legal director at mid-size company
- **Pain**: Managing 50+ external lawyers, no performance visibility
- **Behavior**: Reads legal publications, attends conferences
- **Channel**: LinkedIn, legal tech events, email

### Persona 3: Dr. Ana (B2B - Law Firms)
- **Age**: 30-45
- **Profile**: Partner at growing law firm
- **Pain**: Hard to differentiate from competitors
- **Behavior**: Active on LinkedIn, invests in marketing
- **Channel**: LinkedIn, OAB publications, referrals

## Channel Strategy

### 1. SEO (Primary - Long-term)

**Target Keywords**:
- "melhor advogado [especialidade] [cidade]" (e.g., "melhor advogado trabalhista sao paulo")
- "como escolher um advogado"
- "rating advogados brasil"
- "advogado [nome]" (lawyer name searches)

**Content Strategy**:
- Blog posts on legal rights and procedures
- City-specific lawyer rankings
- "How to choose a lawyer" guides
- Lawyer profile pages (SEO for individual names)

**Technical SEO**:
- Fast, mobile-first site
- Structured data (JSON-LD) for lawyer profiles
- Local SEO optimization

### 2. Content Marketing

**Blog Topics**:
- "5 Perguntas que Voce Deve Fazer Antes de Contratar um Advogado"
- "Entenda Seus Direitos Trabalhistas em 2026"
- "Quanto Custa um Processo Judicial no Brasil?"
- "Como Funciona o Rating de Advogados do CausaGanha"

**Content Calendar**:
- 2x blog posts per week
- 1x infographic per month
- 1x video explainer per quarter

### 3. Social Media

**LinkedIn** (B2B focus):
- Company page: Industry insights, product updates
- Founder content: Thought leadership on legal tech
- Target: Legal directors, HR managers, lawyers

**Instagram** (B2C awareness):
- Infographics on legal rights
- "Did you know" posts
- Success stories (anonymized)
- Target: General audience with potential legal needs

**YouTube**:
- "How to use CausaGanha" tutorials
- "Understanding your rights" explainer series
- Lawyer interview series

### 4. Paid Acquisition (After validation)

**Google Ads**:
- Target: High-intent keywords ("contratar advogado trabalhista")
- Budget: Start with R$ 5K/month, scale with CAC data
- Expected CAC: R$ 30-50

**Meta Ads** (Facebook/Instagram):
- Target: Life events (job loss, divorce, accidents)
- Lookalike audiences from email list
- Retargeting website visitors

### 5. Partnerships

**Legal Marketplaces**:
- GetNinjas, Workana (for legal services)
- White-label API integration

**Insurance Companies**:
- Provide lawyer recommendations for claims
- Enterprise API deals

**OAB Sections**:
- Partnerships for data validation
- Sponsored events

### 6. PR & Media

**Target Publications**:
- Jota (legal news)
- Migalhas (legal professionals)
- Startups Magazine
- Valor Economico (business)

**Story Angles**:
- "Startup uses AI to rate lawyers"
- "Transparency revolution in legal market"
- "Former [founder background] creates lawyer Yelp"

## Launch Campaign

### Pre-Launch (Weeks 1-4)

**Goal**: Build waitlist to 1,000 emails

**Tactics**:
1. Landing page live with email capture
2. Founder posts on LinkedIn (3x/week)
3. Share in legal tech communities
4. Outreach to legal journalists
5. Friends & family network

**KPIs**:
- Waitlist signups: 1,000
- Landing page conversion: 25%+
- Email open rate: 40%+

### Beta Launch (Weeks 5-8)

**Goal**: Get 500 active beta users, collect feedback

**Tactics**:
1. Email waitlist with early access
2. In-app feedback widget
3. User interviews (20+)
4. Iterate on product based on feedback

**KPIs**:
- Beta activation: 50% of waitlist
- Weekly active users: 200+
- NPS: 40+

### Public Launch (Weeks 9-12)

**Goal**: Establish market presence, get press

**Tactics**:
1. Press release to legal publications
2. Product Hunt launch
3. LinkedIn launch campaign
4. Paid ads pilot

**KPIs**:
- Total users: 5,000
- Paid conversions: 100
- Press mentions: 5+

## Competitive Differentiation

### vs. JusBrasil
- They're a search engine, we're a rating platform
- They show documents, we show performance
- They're free, we're freemium with premium features

### vs. Traditional Referrals
- Objective data vs. subjective opinion
- Scalable vs. limited network
- Specialized matching vs. general recommendation

### vs. Law Firm Marketing
- Third-party credibility vs. self-promotion
- Verified results vs. claimed expertise
- Comparative vs. absolute claims

---

## Risk Mitigation

### Risk: OAB Pushback

**Mitigation**:
- Proactive engagement with OAB leadership
- Position as "information service" not "advertising"
- Offer lawyers dispute resolution mechanism
- Get legal opinion from constitutional lawyers

### Risk: Negative PR from Lawyers

**Mitigation**:
- Clear methodology documentation
- Easy dispute resolution process
- Focus messaging on consumer benefit
- Testimonials from lawyers with good ratings

### Risk: Low Awareness

**Mitigation**:
- Aggressive SEO investment
- Strategic PR for credibility
- Referral program for organic growth
- Partnership with high-traffic platforms
