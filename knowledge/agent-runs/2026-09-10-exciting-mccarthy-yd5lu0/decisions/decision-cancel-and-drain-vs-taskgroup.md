rationale: "asyncio.TaskGroup is the more idiomatic Python 3.11+ answer to this exact problem and was seriously considered first (CLAUDE.md already requires Python 3.12+). But TaskGroup re-raises failures wrapped in an ExceptionGroup/BaseExceptionGroup, not the original exception type -- confirmed by reading engine.py's caller chain: download_worker's except clause explicitly catches (httpx.HTTPError, OSError, ValueError, DJENNotFoundError, DJENRateLimitedError) around _stage_download's call to download_zip (engine.py:604-618), and none of those types is ExceptionGroup, nor would a plain `except X` clause match an exception now wrapped inside one without `except*` syntax. Switching to TaskGroup would silently break every one of those catches for the segmented-download path specifically -- an unhandled ExceptionGroup would propagate out of download_worker instead of being logged and the item requeued/failed gracefully, a regression far worse than the bug being fixed. The manual cancel-and-drain wrapper preserves the exact original exception (verified by tests/djen_backup/test_download_zip_segment_cancellation.py asserting `pytest.raises(ValueError, match=...)` and by the pre-existing DJENNotFoundError/httpx.HTTPError-raising tests in test_download_segment.py and test_stage_download.py staying green), while still guaranteeing sibling tasks are cancelled -- the same outcome as TaskGroup without its exception-shape change. # Decisão: cancelar e drenar manualmente, não TaskGroup

`asyncio.TaskGroup` cancelaria os irmãos automaticamente, mas embrulha a falha original num `ExceptionGroup` -- o que quebraria silenciosamente o `except (httpx.HTTPError, OSError, ValueError, DJENNotFoundError, DJENRateLimitedError)` de `engine.py`'s `download_worker`, que hoje depende do tipo de exceção original propagado sem wrapper. Preferido um `try/except BaseException` que cancela, drena e relança a exceção original inalterada.
